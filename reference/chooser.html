Which chart should I use?

The right control chart depends on what you record, how you sample it and how small a change you need to catch. Answer a few questions and the guide names a chart, explains why it fits and points to the module that teaches it.

    The same decisions on one page. Read from left to right, and click a chart to open its module. Small shifts here means a sustained move of about 1.5 standard deviations or less.

    Two cautions apply to every branch. First, a chart is only as good as its Phase I baseline, so set limits from data that describe the process when it runs as it should. Second, check the assumptions behind the limits: roughly normal data for measurement charts, a stable Poisson or binomial process for attribute charts, and independence over time for both.
