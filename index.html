In Control · A free course in statistical process control

Learn to tell a signal from noise.

Twelve interactive modules on control charts, process capability and acceptance sampling, built on chapter 6 of the NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods. Move the sliders, break the process, and watch the charts respond.

How the course works

Read the idea

Short explanations in plain language, with the formulas you need to compute every chart by hand.

Break the process

Every figure is a working simulation. Shift the mean, widen the spread or change the sample size and see what the chart notices.

Check yourself

Each module ends with questions that mark themselves. Progress is kept in your browser, with no account and nothing to install.

The source

NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, chapter 6

The handbook is a public reference written by statisticians at the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology with SEMATECH, the semiconductor manufacturing consortium. This course follows its chapter on process and product monitoring, reuses its worked examples where they help, and links each module back to the original sections so you can go deeper.
